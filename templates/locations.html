Data Structure: Graph (Adjacency List)
BFS: O(V + E) | DFS: O(V + E) | Shortest Path: O(V^2 / E log V)
What is it? A non-linear data structure consisting of vertices $V$ (Locations: Donor Kitchens, Food Banks, Shelters) and edges $E$ (Logistics transport routes with kilometer weights).
Why are we using it? Models the real-world physical supply chain route map for food transport vehicles.
Traversals:
  • BFS (Breadth-First Search): Explores all immediate neighbor hubs level-by-level using our FIFO Queue.
  • DFS (Depth-First Search): Explores transport paths deeply along each route before backtracking.
