Greedy Algorithm: Locally Optimal Strategy
Time: O(n log n) | Space: O(n)
How it works here: There is limited surplus food and multiple competing recipient requests. The Greedy strategy sorts requests by highest Priority (or Urgency-per-Quantity ratio) using custom Merge Sort and allocates food sequentially until capacity runs out. It never reconsiders previous choices.

Backtracking: Constraint Satisfaction & State Search
Time: O(K^M) Worst Case | Space: O(M) Recursion Tree
Problem: Assign $M$ food donors to $K$ collection centers without exceeding any center's intake capacity.
Mechanism: Explores state space recursively. When an assignment violates center capacity, it prunes the branch, backtracks (undoes choice and restores capacity), and tries an alternate center.
