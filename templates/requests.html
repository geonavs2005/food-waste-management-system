Data Structure: FIFO Queue
Enqueue: O(1) | Dequeue: O(1) | Peek: O(1)
What is it? A First-In-First-Out (FIFO) linear data structure where elements are inserted at the rear and removed from the front.
Why are we using it? Guarantees fair, corruption-free sequential processing of surplus food requests based on exact chronological submission time.
How does it work here? Clicking "Process Next Request" executes dequeue() on our custom RequestQueue, serving the front item and advancing the front pointer in O(1) time.
